Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Data Sovereignty

The "Local-First" Principle

The Auditor is built on the philosophy that your financial data is your own.

Hive Storage

By default, all subscription names, costs, and budget details are stored in a local encrypted database (Hive) on your physical device.

Local Service Database:

To ensure absolute privacy, our directory of 440+ subscription templates is bundled directly into the app's code. Searching for or adding a service never sends a ping to an external API or server.

Zero Access

Unless you opt into Cloud Sync, your financial profile never touches our servers.

Information Collection & Usage

Guardian Members

For users utilizing Cloud Sync, encrypted backups are stored to allow for cross-device recovery. This data is used solely for synchronization and is never sold or shared.

Tier Verification

To ensure the integrity of our Premium systems, the app securely verifies the current time via a UTC server check. This process does not collect personal identity markers.

Third-Party Services

AdMob

Standard tier users may see non-personalized advertisements. These services may collect basic device identifiers as governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Payments

All transactions are handled through the Google Play Billing system. We do not store your credit card or banking details.

Hardware Permissions

Notifications

Used exclusively for Scheduled Cancellation alerts and budget milestones.

Biometrics

Used strictly for local device authentication to lock your "Vault." This data stays within the secure enclave of your hardware.

Data Deletion

You retain the right to the "Nuclear Reset." At any time, you may use the in-app settings to permanently purge all local and cloud data associated with your profile.
